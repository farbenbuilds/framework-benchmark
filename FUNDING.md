## Organization

**Farben** ([github.com/farbenbuilds](https://github.com/farbenbuilds)) builds high-performance, low-level systems software, network protocol implementations, and benchmarking infrastructure.

Your support helps fund:
- Dedicated bare-metal benchmark test machines and network hardware.
- Development of open-source high-throughput benchmarking engines.
- Continuous maintenance of framework integrations across diverse languages.
